Designs made for each widget

New: 41 designs shaped by what each widget shows. A temperature becomes a thermometer, fuel a tank, speed a road sign or an aircraft speed tape, the doors a car seen from above. They sit first in each widget's picker, before the 15 generic designs. Each built-in tile carries its own design. In edit mode, tap the palette button on a tile to open the design picker; the choice is saved with that tile, so two clocks can look different. The previews below use sample values; on the head unit every design shows the same live data as today's tile.
